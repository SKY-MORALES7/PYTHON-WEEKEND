Python Weekend is an initiative aimed at introducing beginners to the world of technology and increasing diversity within the tech industry. We do that by organizing workshops and inviting beginners to join us to learn how to build the internet using HTML, CSS, Python and Django in a safe, friendly and inclusive environment.

As a Python Weekend Organizer, you make a commitment to foster and advance Python Weekend values:

  • The workshop you organize will strive to provide safe, inclusive, friendly and positive experience for everyone attending and coaching
  • You will require everyone involved with the workshop to comply with Python Weekend Code of Conduct
  • You'll strive to have tons of fun while organizing your amazing workshop! 🙌
