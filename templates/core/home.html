What is Python Weekend?

Python Weekend was created to give complete beginners a practical, supportive entry into programming. The programme focuses on Python foundations and introduces beginner artificial intelligence concepts.

During a workshop, participants build their first working project in a small group guided by a mentor. The central Python Weekend team provides local organisers with the curriculum, training resources and community support needed to deliver a strong learning experience.
